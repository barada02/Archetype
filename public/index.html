How it works

From title to partner meeting in three steps

1Name the releasePick the film or show, your target age and gender, and the city you are opening in.
2Archetype measures the audienceQloo supplies every number: who the fans are, which brands and artists they over-index on, where they cluster. A language model then picks from Qloo's ranked candidates and explains each choice.
3Take the brief into the roomShare a link or print it. Every claim traces back to the Qloo call behind it, so you can defend each partner.
What's in a brief

Everything a partnerships lead needs on one page

Partner shortlistUp to eight brands across fashion, food and drink, beauty, travel, automotive and gaming, with a fit score and a reason for each.
AudienceMeasured age and gender skew, genres, moods and traits, plus co-marketing angles grounded in them.
Trailer and soundtrackMainstream artists for reach and emerging ones that are cheaper to license.
Screenings and timingVenues and neighborhoods where fans cluster, and the weekly interest trend into launch.
Plain AI vs dataWhat a plain AI would pitch with no data, scored against the same audience next to the shortlist.
SourcesEvery Qloo call behind every number: endpoint, parameters, status and timing.
