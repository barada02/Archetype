## Title

Archetype — co-marketing briefs for film and TV releases, grounded in the Qloo taste graph

## What it does

Name a film or show. Archetype measures its real audience in the Qloo taste graph and builds a
one-page brief for the partnerships lead who has to sign co-marketing deals: a ranked partner
shortlist across fashion, food and drink, beauty, travel, automotive and gaming (each brand checked
against its own customers), measured audience skew with co-marketing angles, mainstream and emerging
soundtrack artists, screening venues and neighborhoods for the opening city, and whether interest is
rising into launch. Each partner gets a one-click outreach email drafted only from the brief's data.

## What makes it Qloo-powered

A plain language model guesses who the audience is — in our tests it contradicted the measured
demographics, could not say whether a title was trending, and pitched partners that scored below the
data-backed picks. Archetype's agentic pipeline calls Qloo for every number (demographics, tags,
cross-domain affinities, heatmaps, trends, shortlist ranking), lets the model only pick from Qloo's
ranked candidates and explain them, then validates the output: invented partners are dropped and any
text with a number the data did not give is replaced. The "Plain AI vs data" tab proves the gap live
by scoring an unaided model's guesses against the same audience, and the Sources tab shows the
agent's own trace behind every claim. Remove Qloo and the product is an empty page.

## How to try it

Open the demo, click "See a sample brief" for instant output, or build a brief for any title in
about 30 seconds. No login needed.
